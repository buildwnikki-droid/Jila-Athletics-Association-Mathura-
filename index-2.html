About the Association

Building Mathura's athletics culture

The Jila Athletics Association Mathura is the district-level athletics body responsible for organizing athletics meets, youth track and field championships, selection trials, grassroots sporting activities, and promoting physical fitness and athletic talent across Mathura district.

The association works to encourage grassroots participation, discipline, physical fitness, athletic development, competitive readiness, talent identification, and pathways to state and national competition.

The Pathway

From discovery to the international stage

The route an athlete may follow as they progress through competitive levels.

01

Discover

Talent is identified through grassroots and school-level participation.

02

District Trial

Athletes take part in district-level selection trials.

03

District Championship

Competing at the district athletics championships.

04

State Selection

Selected athletes progress to state-level trials.

05

State Championship

Representing the district at state championships.

06

National Level

Competing at national athletics competitions.

07

International Stage

Representing India at international junior/senior competitions.
